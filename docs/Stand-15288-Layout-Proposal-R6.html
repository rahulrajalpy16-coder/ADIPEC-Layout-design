1 · The current booth what the files show

  • Size and type. 6 m wide × 5 m deep, 30 m², peninsula. Front, left and right aisles are open; the rear adjoins Stand 15285. 10 cm raised platform with blue edge light. Overall height 400 cm.
  • Back wall. 600 × 330 cm with two 250 × 200 cm screens and two 130 × 35 cm lit shelves below them. From R2 the two screens are replaced by an 8-module video wall on the same outer footprint (see the placement schedule).
  • Header. A 600 × 260 cm rear header frame (60 cm fascia, open centre) carrying the Sinor Marine Services logo on the left side and the Amphicos logo on the right side. A semicircular canopy (Ø 465 cm, 230 cm deep) projects to the front with a 700 × 50 cm wrap-around flexible screen and a ceiling of downlights.
  • Front corners. Two angled 165 × 330 cm frames with blue LED edges, double-sided: Sinor Marine on the left, Amphicos on the right, each with a 55" TV and a 50 × 35 cm laptop shelf on the aisle face.
  • Columns. One triple-pole column with a 75 × 150 cm lightbox on each side, about 1.75 m from the rear.
  • Reception. Curved 170 × 50 × 95 cm counter at front centre carrying the Sinor Marine and Amphicos logos, with lockable storage.
  • Seating. Two round tables with three chairs each under the canopy, inside a Ø 465 cm blue floor ring. Two potted plants appear in the renders near the rear sides.
  • Design language. White surfaces, Amphicos cyan, Sinor navy, blue LED edge lighting and lit acrylic logos.
  • Power. B = 60 A three-phase main DB at rear left (1.0 m from the left side, 0.5 m from the rear). A = 15 A single-phase build-up power at rear right. All cabling runs under the platform.
  • Fixed vs movable. Walls, header, canopy, frames, columns and screens are structural and stay. Tables, chairs, plants and the small side shelves can move.

5 · Decisions for you

D1 · Console positions
Recommended: rear-left (MT) and rear-right (NAVIS) corners, as drawn.
Or send the marked drawing the requirements refer to.
Changed in R6. From the client's marked-up plan, NAVIS moves to right of centre (centre 410 cm from the left, 192 cm from the rear) in place of the right seating group's rear chair. MT stays in the rear-left corner.
D2 · Console orientation
Recommended: turned 45° toward the booth centre, so the screens read from the side aisle and the front diagonal.
Alternative: square to the front aisle. Tidier lines, but the screens face the back of the frames.
Changed in R6 for NAVIS. At its new position NAVIS is square to the front aisle with the screen facing visitors. MT stays at 45°.
D3 · Which side gets Fugro and Sonardyne
Recommended: Fugro with MT on the Sinor Marine side, Sonardyne with NAVIS on the Amphicos side.
Alternative: swap them. The document does not specify sides.
Superseded in R6. Fugro and Sonardyne share one table on the left (Sinor Marine) edge: Sonardyne on the rear half, Fugro on the front half. The right edge is empty.
D4 · MT and NAVIS logo fixing
Recommended: lit acrylic panels hung from the header frame directly over each console.
Alternative: put them on the white header corners for front-aisle visibility. This moves Fugro and Sonardyne onto table-mounted risers instead.
Superseded in R5. The hung panels blocked the video wall and were removed. Each logo is now on a lit white fascia (≈ 58 × 40 cm) across its console's front cabinet, 20–60 cm above the platform. Fugro and Sonardyne are on their table fronts (R2) and no longer on the header corners (R3); the header corners stay blank.
D5 · Display tables
Recommended: two 100 × 50 × 95 cm white plinth tables with blue LED edge to match the counter, with a lockable cradle for SPRINT-Nav.
Sizes change once the unit datasheets arrive.
Superseded in R6. One 150 × 50 × 95 cm table on the left edge carries both units, Sonardyne in its lockable acrylic cradle. The right-edge table is removed.
D6 · SMT logo
Recommended: counter front, centred between Sinor Marine and Amphicos.
Alternative: beside the SMS logo on the left side of the header.
Superseded in R2. The SMT logo is on the back wall, centred below the video wall (≈ 90 × 36 cm, centre 80 cm). The counter keeps only the Sinor Marine Services and Amphicos logos. SMT also appears in the Partner Brands Showcase.
Superseded in R6. The back-wall SMT logo is removed. SMT appears only in the Partner Brands Showcase.
D7 · Seating
Recommended: keep both tables, shift ~25 cm forward, three chairs each.
Fallback: two chairs per table.
Changed in R6. Left group as recommended. The right group moves a further 35 cm forward (~60 cm in all) with two chairs, outer side and front; its rear chair gives way to NAVIS.
