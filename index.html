Getting around

Orbit

Drag to rotate, right-drag or two fingers to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.

Walk

WASD or arrows to move, Shift to go faster. Click the view to look with the mouse, Esc to release. Scroll to zoom. On phones, use the left stick and drag to look.

Inspect

Click any numbered item or pick it from Equipment. The camera flies to it and shows its details.

VR

On a WebXR headset browser such as Meta Quest, press Enter VR. Left stick moves, right stick turns.
